1. The BioDex Service and Licensing

BioDex is an interactive wildlife tracking platform. It allows users to log flora and fauna sightings using the HTML5 Geolocation API, populating a community-driven Leaflet map for research and education. Gamified elements like "Discovery Badges" are virtual and hold no real-world value.

Your privacy is important to us. We only access your GPS coordinates when you tap to log a sighting. We do not track your background movements or sell your personal data.
